About Me

Josh McGuigan is an astrophotographer and tour guide, based in the Mackenzie Country of New Zealand's South Island. When the conditions aren't ideal for shooting the night sky, you can find him capturing New Zealand's dramatic landscapes — or getting ready for the next big mission. Available now for work in New Zealand and worldwide.
